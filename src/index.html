About this page

All data is based on the data available through the real-time information page. Source: Lokaltrafiken Kiruna.

This page is not affiliated with either Kiruna Kommun or Boreal. It is provided as is, primarily for educational purposes. Data may be delayed or inaccurate.

Tåg (station shuttle), Flyg (airport shuttle) and Tom (empty runs) are off by default. Dashed Tåg and Flyg routes are approximate and may not follow roads.
